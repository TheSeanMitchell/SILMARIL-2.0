WHAT THIS FIXES (your exact questions):

Q: "The leaderboards did not reset — same champions on top, will they ever rotate?"
A: Two different systems that the UI never distinguished:
   - ARENA LEADERBOARD = a BACKTEST over price history. Those 38-478 "trades" are how each
     strategy WOULD HAVE done on real historical prices. It SURVIVES WIPES on purpose (price
     history survives wipes). It is a hypothesis generator, not live results. Refreshes hourly.
   - CHAMPION ELECTION = reads your LIVE paper-book trades (currently 0 after the wipe). No live
     trades -> survivability unproven -> incumbent holds. THIS is what rotates.

Q: "When will it reset champion trades?"
A: They reset the moment you wipe (they are already 0 in this backup). The BACKTEST numbers
   don't reset because they aren't live trades — they're history-based hypotheses.

Q: "When will it rotate more than 5 strategies?"
A: It already backtests the FULL population (72 crypto strategies) every hourly cycle — the UI was
   just capping the DISPLAY at 8. FIXED: now shows the full ranked field (top 25 visible + scroll,
   with "N strategies competing" and the total). Nothing was ever only-5; you just couldn't see it.

Q: "How often will the champion rotate per quadrant?"
A: HOURLY, once live trades exist. Precisely: every hourly cycle the engine re-scores forward
   survivability from live paper trades; a challenger becomes champion when it beats the incumbent
   by >=15 survivability points AND has >=5 live closed trades (anti-flip-flop hysteresis — slow on
   purpose so you trade real edge, not noise). Until crypto arms (~2h post-wipe) and trades accrue,
   the champion correctly HOLDS.
