# 13 · THE BRAIN TAB — nothing is decoration, and now the machine proves it

## Why this exists
The recurring wound: metrics kept getting measured and then silently disconnected — "progress we
keep thinking we are making seems to be disconnected somewhere in the process." The BRAIN tab plus
`brain_wiring.py` end that class of problem structurally, not by promise.

## The seven panels (tab 🧠, between COMMAND and STRATEGY)

**🧠→👑 MASTER BRAIN** — the confidence engine's strongest leans per industry, the exact numbers
that size every wager and gate the sniper. This is the feed the Golden Master follows; it earns
trading rights only through the 90-day bar, but what it will follow is visible now.

**🪙 DECISION FLOW — THE COIN MACHINE** — every cycle, every industry: seen → warm → candidates →
bought, with every rejection NAMED and counted, and animated coins for each buy. This is the
decision path itself rendered, not a picture of it.

**🔌 SIGNAL LEDGER** — every signal store: green light (exists + real consumers), its key metric
NOW, a ▲/▼ shift arrow vs the last run, and exactly which files consume it feeding which decision.
**The consumer map is verified by selftest T22 against the real file contents — this table cannot
lie.** A signal with no consumer renders a red DECORATION light, and the battery fails before that
can ever ship.

**🎛 CONFIDENCE ANATOMY** — the top names with all 9 components of their score as bars (bounce
reliability, rhythm regularity, rhythm phase, MTF confluence, dip extension, timing alignment,
momentum exhaustion, conviction backing, trend). The anatomy of every sizing decision.

**🔮 DR. STRANGE — SELF-GRADED** — the discovery of this build: Dr. Strange was ALREADY grading
itself (career: picks, resolved, hit-rate) but nothing consumed the grades. Now its own career
feeds the experimental gate (resolved/50, hit-rate printed on the gate row) and the full pick
history renders here. Trust earned by forward accuracy only.

**📁 SYMBOL DOSSIER** — pick any open position or leader: price chart with ▲peak/▼trough markers,
entry/target/stop lines when held, LAST PEAK, **NEXT-PEAK ETA** (last peak + median cycle),
typical swing + cycle length, **bounce likelihood**, its own buy level, momentum trajectory
(h1/d1/d2/w1), MTF confluence, confidence with all 9 component bars, and the plain-English why.
Everything the system knows about one name, one screen.

**👁 ALL CHARTS, ONE EYESHOT** — every open position as a mini-chart with entry·target·stop
overlaid and live uPL%. The whole book at a glance.

## The engine behind it — `brain_wiring.py` → `BRAIN_WIRING.json`
Runs every cycle on the spine. Inventories 14 signal stores, checks existence + freshness, records
each key metric and its delta vs the previous cycle (the shift arrows), and builds the dossiers
(compact series + rhythm/likelihood/trajectory facts) for open positions ∪ confidence leaders ∪
rhythm leaders. Measures and displays only — changes no behavior.

## The permanent guards
- **T22** — every consumer listed for every signal must actually contain the store's filename.
- **T25** — the BRAIN tab, guide, sections, and renderer must exist.
- **T26** — the dossier contract (peaks · ETA · likelihoods · trajectory · anatomy · master brain).
Break any wiring in the future and the battery fails before install.
