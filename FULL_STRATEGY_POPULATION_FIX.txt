SILMARIL 3.0 — FULL STRATEGY POPULATION FIX (arena shows all 316)

YOUR QUESTION: "The arena is supposed to have ~250 strategies. Are we getting the full
250 or did something get lost?"

ANSWER: Nothing was lost — the population is actually 316 (bigger than the ~250 you
remembered). But it was being SAMPLED and TRUNCATED in three places, so you only ever
saw a slice. All three are now fixed.

WHAT WAS HAPPENING (verified by counting the actual code):
  - The strategy grid builder makes TWO sizes:
      compact = 72 strategies (a coarse MR sample: 4 drops x 3 targets x 3 stops + full
                MOM/PERSIST/HOLD/patient families)
      wide    = 316 strategies (full catalog: 8 drops x 7 targets x 5 stops = 280 MR
                + 12 MOM + 4 PERSIST + 18 HOLD + 2 patient)
  - The HOURLY arena (what the UI shows) used the 72-compact grid — to stay fast.
  - The DAILY wide arena bred all 316 but WROTE ONLY THE TOP 120 to its file (ranked[:120]).
  - So: 316 always competed for best-trusted/champion, but the DISPLAY showed 72 (hourly)
    or 120 (wide). The ~250 you remembered = the wide MR grid (now 280 MR / 316 total).

THE FIX (3 changes, all verified):
  1. strategy_lab.py — the module-level STRATEGIES now uses the FULL 316-grid. Timing test
     proved backtesting all 316 across all 4 books takes ~6-17s, well within the hourly
     budget. (The old July-1 cron balloons were the news/IPO builders — already gated off
     the fast cycle — NEVER this grid.) So the hourly arena now competes ALL 316 per book.
  2. strategy_lab.py — the wide arena no longer truncates to [:120]; it writes the full
     ranked field and adds a `competed`/`grid_size` count (316).
  3. index.html — the Arena tab now shows the full ranked field (top 25 visible + scroll),
     displays "316 strategies competing", labels the board as BACKTEST, and shows the
     live-trades rotation banner. champion.py emits live_trades so rotation progress shows.

CADENCE SAFETY: the heavy arena runs at most once per ~45 min (committed timestamp gate);
the 10-minute trade cycle is untouched. No stacking risk. Verified.

INSTALL: drag-drop the 3 files (replace), commit. No wipe needed — this is grid size +
display + one JSON field. After the next hourly cycle, every quadrant board shows all 316
nicknamed strategies competing, per book, on its own universe.
