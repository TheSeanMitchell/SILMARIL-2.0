## THE 7.0 LAWS — the go-live constitution
21. **ABSTAIN, NEVER DISTORT.** No floor, default, or override may silently replace a measured per-name value. If the honest number makes a trade unwinnable, the system refuses the TRADE — it never fakes the number. *(This is the law the old stop-floor broke for two months. Enforced: geometry gate, T44.)*
22. **THE LAW OF WINNABLE TRADES.** Every entry computes p\* = (stop+cost)/(target+stop) and compares it to the Wilson evidence floor (own record ∪ cluster prior, weight stated). Demand > proof ⇒ no trade, reason written. (T44)
23. **A SCORE THAT CANNOT PREDICT CANNOT ALLOCATE.** Confidence is graded every cycle (Brier + reliability). QUARANTINE strips its gating authority until re-earned. (T47)
24. **THE GOVERNOR'S HAND IS PRE-REGISTERED.** Drawdown ladder GREEN×1.0 / AMBER×0.5+harvest / RED×0-halt, daily-loss and streak breakers — no discretion, ever. (T48)
25. **CRYPTO IS ONE BET.** Aggregate crypto+GEKKO exposure is capped; ten alts are one position wearing ten names. (T48)
26. **LEARNING IS PERMANENT.** No store may truncate without archiving the evicted rows to `docs/data/archive/*.jsonl.gz`. A small repo means compressed history, never discarded history. (T49)
27. **PILLAGE WITH A NAME.** Concentration on a proven single source is PERMITTED — when it is named on the dashboard, sized by the ladder, and survives the remove-the-top-name test. Finding the one unlocked door and robbing the house is the job; doing it blind is the crime.
28. **ALPHA WITH INSTRUMENTS.** *(Replaces the no-new-alpha law.)* New strategies are welcome the moment the measuring rod exists: every new signal enters as a lab sleeve, races the nulls, and earns capital through the same gates as everything else. Ideas were never the bottleneck; unmeasured ideas were.
29. **DATA FLIPS KNOBS, CODE DOES NOT.** Every graduation (edge-surface observe→gate, calibration authority, crash re-entry, live unlock) is a pre-registered threshold that flips a knob and writes `_armed_at`. After 7.0, evolution is a data event. (edge_surface self-arm is the reference implementation)
30. **GENESIS.** The operator may burn it all down — books, sleeves, Master, AND learning — via the registry-driven genesis wipe. Archives alone are sacred. A true clean start is always one workflow away. (T51)
